Learning what to look at

Attention lets a model, when producing each part of its output, look back over the entire input and decide which parts are most relevant right now — rather than being forced to compress everything into one fixed-size summary.

Origins in machine translation

Bahdanau, Cho, and Bengio introduced attention in 2014 to improve neural machine translation, letting a translating model align each output word with the relevant words in the source sentence, dramatically improving translation of long sentences.

Self-attention

The real leap came with self-attention: letting every position in a sequence attend to every other position in that same sequence, capturing relationships regardless of distance.

From add-on to foundation

What started as an enhancement bolted onto recurrent networks turned out to be powerful enough to replace recurrence altogether.

Setting up the Transformer

That realization — that attention alone was enough — set the stage for the next breakthrough in this tree.

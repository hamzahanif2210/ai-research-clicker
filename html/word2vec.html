Meaning as geometry

word2vec, introduced by Tomas Mikolov and colleagues at Google in 2013, learns to represent each word as a dense vector of numbers, trained so that words appearing in similar contexts end up nearby in vector space.

The famous result: vector("king") - vector("man") + vector("woman") lands close to vector("queen"). Word embeddings became a foundational building block for nearly every subsequent NLP system, letting models work with meaning rather than raw text.

Scale changes everything

By training Transformer-based models on ever larger datasets with ever more parameters, researchers found that language models developed increasingly general capabilities — few-shot learning, reasoning, coding, and conversation — often without being explicitly trained for them.

These Large Language Models (LLMs), and the broader class of "foundation models" they belong to, are now adapted to a huge range of downstream tasks through fine-tuning or simply by prompting — a shift from building one model per task to building one model for (almost) everything.

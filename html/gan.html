You discovered Generative Adversarial Networks!

A forger and a detective

Proposed by Ian Goodfellow and colleagues in 2014, a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) pits two networks against each other: a generator that tries to create convincing fake data, and a discriminator that tries to tell fakes from real examples. As they compete, the generator gets remarkably good at producing realistic images, audio, and more.

GANs kicked off a wave of research into generative modeling and were, for years, the state of the art in photorealistic image synthesis, later joined and in many applications surpassed by diffusion models.

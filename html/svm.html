Support Vector Machines

Support Vector Machines (SVMs) classify data by finding the hyperplane that separates two classes with the widest possible margin. Using the "kernel trick," SVMs can also draw non-linear decision boundaries by implicitly mapping data into a higher-dimensional space.

Throughout the 1990s and 2000s, SVMs were among the most reliable and widely used tools in applied machine learning, excelling at tasks like text classification and bioinformatics before neural networks came to dominate.

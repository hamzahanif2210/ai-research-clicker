AlexNet and the 2012 breakthrough

In 2012, Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever, and Geoffrey Hinton entered the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge with a deep CNN trained on GPUs, later known as AlexNet. It beat the next best entry by a huge margin, and the deep learning era began in earnest.

The combination of large labeled datasets (ImageNet), affordable parallel compute (GPUs), and better training techniques (ReLU activations, dropout) turned neural networks from a niche technique into the dominant approach across computer vision, and soon much of the rest of machine learning.

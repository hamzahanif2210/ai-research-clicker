You discovered Backpropagation!

Teaching every layer at once

Backpropagation is the algorithm that made deep, multi-layer neural networks trainable. It applies the chain rule from calculus to efficiently compute how much each individual weight in a network contributed to the final error, and nudges every weight accordingly.

A rediscovery whose time had come

The mathematics behind backpropagation had been described in various forms since the 1960s and 70s, but it was the 1986 paper by Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams that showed it could train multi-layer networks to discover useful internal representations, sparking a wave of new interest in neural networks.

What is this training visualizer?

Think of the canvas in the middle of the game as a live training run: every click sends a fresh batch of data through your model, and the tracks you see represent gradients and activations flowing through its layers as it learns. Just as a particle detector reconstructs invisible collisions from the tracks they leave behind, this visualizer reconstructs what's happening inside a network from the signals it produces.

Why visualize training at all?

Neural networks are famously hard to inspect directly — a model can have billions of parameters. Researchers rely on visualizations like loss curves, gradient flows, attention maps, and activation plots to build intuition for what a model is doing, spot problems early, and communicate results to others.

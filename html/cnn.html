Teaching computers to see

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) slide small, learnable filters across an image to detect local patterns like edges, textures, and shapes. Stacking many convolutional layers lets a network build up from simple edges to complex objects.

LeNet and the first real-world deployment

In the late 1980s and 1990s, Yann LeCun and colleagues developed LeNet, a CNN trained to recognize handwritten digits. It was deployed to read handwritten numbers on bank cheques — one of the earliest commercial successes of a neural network.

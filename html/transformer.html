Attention Is All You Need

In 2017, a team at Google published "Attention Is All You Need," introducing the Transformer: an architecture built entirely from self-attention layers, with no recurrence at all. It trained faster, scaled better, and modeled long-range relationships more effectively than RNNs or LSTMs.

The Transformer became the backbone of nearly every major advance in NLP and beyond that followed — BERT, GPT, and the large language models that came after all descend from this one architecture.
